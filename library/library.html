What Scrollback does

Scrollback reads the message lists your Discord tab already downloads and keeps a copy in this browser. It sends no requests to Discord of its own. It never reads your login token or your request headers.

History jobs (7d, 30d, 90d, All) scroll the chat for you, the same way you would by hand. Server jobs open each text channel in the sidebar one at a time.

Privacy

Everything stays in this browser profile. Scrollback has no server, no analytics and no remote code. Nothing leaves your device unless you download or copy it. Delete one chat or all data at any time from this page; removing the extension also removes the data.

Limits

  • Messages that arrive live while a chat is open are saved the next time Discord reloads that chat.
  • Edits and deletions after a message was saved do not update the saved copy until Discord loads it again.
  • Discord attachment links expire after about a day. Save media soon after you load a chat.
  • Only export chats you have the right to keep. Respect the people in them and Discord's Terms.
